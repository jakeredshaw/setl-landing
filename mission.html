Screen time and our mission: FAQs

What is SETL's mission?

A billion hours given back. SETL blocks the apps that take your evenings so that time returns to sleep, to focus and to the people in the room.

What is the SETL manifesto?

That attention is not a character flaw. Feeds are built to hold you, willpower is lowest at night, so the decision should be made earlier and enforced for you.

How much screen time does SETL save?

In SETL's 2026 beta, testers reported an average of 4 hours 3 minutes less screen time a day: about 3 hours in the day and 1 hour at night.

How much screen time do adults have?

Ofcom found UK adults spent 4 hours 30 minutes a day online in 2025, with 77% of that time on a smartphone.

Does screen time in bed affect sleep?

Research links it. A 2025 study of 45,202 students found each extra hour of screen use in bed was linked to about 24 minutes less sleep.

Does SETL collect my data?

No. SETL uses Apple's Screen Time framework, which gives it private tokens, so we never see which apps you block. Read our privacy policy.
