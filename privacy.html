Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

SETL is built privacy-first. We designed it so your most personal information — when you sleep and how you use your phone — stays on your phone. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what we collect, what we do not, and the choices you have.

The short version. The SETL app has no accounts and no servers. What you set up in the app (your name, sleep schedule, chosen apps and preferences) is stored on your device and is not sent to us. Your Screen Time data never leaves your phone. The only information we receive is the email address you choose to give us on our waitlist.

1. Who we are

SETL ("SETL", "we", "us", "our") is a sleep-accountability app operated by Jacob Redshaw, based in England, United Kingdom. This policy covers the SETL website (including this page and our waitlist) and the SETL iOS app.

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at jacob.redshaw13@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Our website and waitlist

  • Email address — only if you submit it to join our waitlist. We use it to let you know when SETL is available and to send occasional updates about the launch.
  • Basic technical data — like any website, our host may automatically log standard request information (such as your IP address and browser type) to serve the page and keep it secure. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which means those providers may receive your IP address when the fonts are requested.

Our website does not use advertising pixels, analytics trackers, or non-essential cookies.

The SETL app

When you set SETL up, you provide information such as your name, your wake and bedtimes, the apps you want to rest at night, your general location or time zone, your email address, and your appearance preferences. This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us. SETL currently has no account system and no backend server that receives it.

  • Screen Time data — SETL uses Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to block apps at bedtime and to nudge you if you are still on your phone. Apple provides SETL with opaque tokens that represent your chosen apps; this data is handled on your device by Apple's system and is never sent to us. We cannot see your browsing, messages, or the content of your activity.
  • Notifications — SETL's reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. They do not require, and do not use, a push server.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising — the app contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no tracking.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the app's features on your device (protecting your sleep window, blocking and unblocking apps, sending your reminders and your Lock Screen widget).
  • To contact you about the SETL waitlist and launch, if you gave us your email.
  • To keep our website and services secure and working correctly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Legal bases (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — when you give us your email to join the waitlist. You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to remove you.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep our website secure and functioning, provided this does not override your rights.

5. Who we share it with

We keep sharing to the minimum needed to run SETL:

  • Google — waitlist email addresses submitted on our site are stored in Google Sheets via Google Apps Script. Google acts as our processor. Google Fonts may also receive your IP address when our pages load.
  • Fontshare — supplies one of the typefaces used on our site and may receive your IP address when our pages load.
  • Apple — the app runs on Apple's operating system and uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks; if you subscribe in future, payments are processed by Apple, and we never receive your card details.
  • Our website host — which serves the site and may keep standard security logs.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of SETL and its users.

6. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist emails until you ask us to delete them or until they are no longer needed for the launch, after which we remove them. Information you enter in the app lives on your device; deleting the app, or using the app's "Delete account" option, removes that local data.

7. Security

Because the app keeps your setup on your device rather than on our servers, there is no central store of your personal sleep and usage data for anyone to breach. For the information we do hold (waitlist emails), we rely on reputable providers and reasonable safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is ever completely secure, but we take your privacy seriously.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email jacob.redshaw13@gmail.com and we will respond within the time required by law.

If you are in the UK, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. If you are in the EU, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

SETL is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Some of our providers (such as Google and Apple) are based in, or process data in, the United States and other countries. Where information is transferred outside the UK or EEA, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the providers' standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SETL grows — for example, if we add accounts or subscriptions. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will make it clear on our website.

12. Contact

Questions, requests, or anything at all about your privacy: jacob.redshaw13@gmail.com.
