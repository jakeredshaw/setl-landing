How to block Instagram at night on your iPhone

You open Instagram to check one message and surface forty minutes later. Here is how to shut it off at bedtime, and why the in app settings are not enough.

By Jacob Redshaw · 14 September 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

To block Instagram at night on iPhone, add a Screen Time Schedule in iOS 27, or turn on Downtime with Block at Downtime in iOS 26, covering bedtime to wake time. Instagram's own sleep mode pauses notifications, but Instagram says most people can still use the app during it.

If you want to block Instagram at night, the settings you need are already on your phone. Instagram has its own night tool too, but it does something narrower than most people assume, so it is worth knowing the difference before you rely on it.

A night block means Instagram will not open between a set evening time and a set morning time. A daily limit is a different thing. It can be spent by lunchtime and then waved away.

How to block Instagram at night on iPhone

Apple changed the names of these settings in iOS 27. Use the section that matches your phone.

iOS 27: Screen Time Schedule

In iOS 27, Screen Time uses Schedules for when you can use apps and Time Allowances for how long.1

  1. Open Settings, then tap Screen Time.
  2. Tap Screen Time Schedule, then turn on Schedule.1
  3. Tap Add Schedule, tap the days of the week you want, then confirm.
  4. Tap the Time Window, rename it to something like Night, then set the start and stop times.1
  5. Choose which Time Allowance categories stay available in that window, and leave Social Media out.
  6. Tap Always Allowed so calls and anything you truly need still work.1

Apple notes that Schedules and Time Allowances need iOS 27 or later on all your Apple devices.1

iOS 26: Downtime and App Limits

On iOS 26, Downtime does the scheduling and App Limits do the daily caps.2

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then tap App & Website Activity and turn it on if it is off.
  2. Tap Downtime, then tap Scheduled.
  3. Choose Every Day or Customize Days, then set your start and end times.2
  4. Go back into Downtime and turn on Block at Downtime.2
Do not skip this: Apple says that by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once reached. With Block at Downtime on, restricted apps cannot be opened during downtime and appear dimmed with an hourglass icon.2

Use a Screen Time passcode you do not use elsewhere. If you keep tapping through your own limits, read why Screen Time limits stop working.

What does Instagram sleep mode actually do?

Instagram has a night setting, now called sleep mode. Find it at Profile, then the Menu button in the top right, then Settings and activity, then Time management, then Sleep mode. You turn it on and pick the days and times.3

Instagram says sleep mode automatically pauses your notifications, auto replies to messages and lets people know you are in sleep mode during the periods you choose, and that you will see a reminder to close Instagram.3 Your activity status changes to In sleep mode and the green active dot becomes a moon icon.3

Here is the sentence that matters. Instagram states that for most people, when you are in sleep mode, you can still use Instagram and send and receive messages.3 So it is a quiet sign on the door, not a lock. Teen Accounts are treated differently: Instagram says they are defaulted into sleep mode from 22:00 to 07:00 every day, and a parent or guardian may also choose to block Instagram during sleep mode.3

The daily limit is the same kind of tool. Instagram describes it as a reminder to let you know when you have spent a certain amount of time on Instagram during the day, set under Time management, then Daily limit.4

One more thing to expect. Instagram says that when sleep mode ends you get a summary of the notifications and messages you missed.3 That is a pile waiting for you in the morning, which is worth planning for.

Why Instagram Reels at night is so hard to leave

Instagram describes Reels as short, entertaining videos to create, watch and share, with a dedicated Reels tab and a space it calls somewhere to encounter shareable moments as they happen.5 Reels can run up to 20 minutes of recorded clips, although Instagram says reels over three minutes will not be recommended to new audiences.5

Why a night block beats a daily limit

Night is when the cost shows up. In a study of 45,202 Norwegian university students aged 18 to 28, each extra hour of screen use after going to bed was linked to 59 percent higher odds of reporting insomnia symptoms and to 24 minutes less sleep. The link was not significantly different for social media than for other screen activities, so it is the time in bed that counts, not only the app.6

The phone is usually within reach. In an online survey of 2,005 US adults for the American Academy of Sleep Medicine in March 2023, 87 percent said they regularly sleep with their phone in their bedroom.7 The NHS suggests trying not to use phones, tablets or computers for an hour before bed.8

Firmer limits also do more than reminders. In the randomised experiment behind the paper Digital Addiction, the authors report that allowing people to set limits on their future screen time substantially reduces use, and estimate that self control problems account for 31 percent of social media use.9

Block Instagram at night: FAQs

How do I block Instagram at night on my iPhone?

On iOS 27, go to Settings, Screen Time, Screen Time Schedule and add a night window. On iOS 26, go to Settings, Screen Time, Downtime, set it to Scheduled, then turn on Block at Downtime.

Does Instagram sleep mode block the app?

No. Instagram says sleep mode pauses notifications, auto replies to messages and shows a reminder to close the app, but that most people can still use Instagram while it is on.

Where is sleep mode in the Instagram app?

Tap Profile, then the Menu button in the top right, then Settings and activity, then Time management, then Sleep mode. Turn on the toggle and pick your days and times.

Does the Instagram daily limit stop me using Instagram?

No. Instagram describes it as a reminder that tells you when you have spent a chosen amount of time on Instagram that day. It is under Time management, then Daily limit.

Why do Instagram Reels keep me up at night?

Reels is a running feed of short videos in its own tab, so one tap takes you from checking a message to watching. Nothing in the feed tells you when to stop.

Is blocking Instagram at night better than a daily limit?

Usually, yes. A daily limit can be used up early and then ignored. A night block covers the hours when scrolling costs you sleep, and it repeats every night without a decision from you.
