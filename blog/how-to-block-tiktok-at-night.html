How to block TikTok at night on your iPhone

One more video, then it is 1am. Here is how to shut TikTok off at bedtime using Apple's settings, TikTok's settings, and what to do when neither holds.

By Jacob Redshaw · 14 September 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

To block TikTok at night on iPhone, set a Screen Time Schedule in iOS 27, or turn on Downtime with Block at Downtime in iOS 26, covering bedtime to wake time. TikTok's own sleep reminders only mute notifications and can be dismissed, so they will not stop you opening the app.

If you want to block TikTok at night, you are not short on willpower. You are up against a feed built to keep going. The good news is that your iPhone can close the app for you, and it takes about five minutes to set up.

A night block is simple. TikTok stops opening from a set time in the evening until a set time in the morning. That is different from a daily limit, which you can burn through by lunchtime and then ignore for the rest of the day.

How to block TikTok at night on iPhone

Apple renamed these settings in iOS 27. Use whichever section matches your phone.

iOS 27: Screen Time Schedule

In iOS 27, Screen Time uses Schedules to set when you can use apps and Time Allowances to set how long.1

  1. Open Settings, then tap Screen Time.
  2. Tap Screen Time Schedule, then turn on Schedule.1
  3. Tap Add Schedule, tap the days of the week you want, then confirm.
  4. Tap the Time Window, rename it something like Night, then set the start and stop times.1
  5. Choose which Time Allowance categories stay available in that window. Leave Social Media out of it.
  6. Tap Always Allowed and check that calls and anything you genuinely need at night are still there.1

Apple notes that Schedules and Time Allowances need iOS 27 or later on all your Apple devices.1

iOS 26: Downtime and App Limits

On iOS 26 the same job is done by Downtime. App Limits set the daily caps.2

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then tap App & Website Activity and turn it on if it is off.
  2. Tap Downtime, then tap Scheduled.
  3. Choose Every Day or Customize Days, then set your start and end times.2
  4. Go back into Downtime and turn on Block at Downtime.2
Do not skip this: Apple says that by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once reached. With Block at Downtime on, restricted apps cannot be opened during downtime and appear dimmed with an hourglass icon.2

Set a Screen Time passcode you do not use anywhere else. If you already know the code by heart, read why Screen Time limits stop working, or our fuller guide to setting up Downtime on iPhone.

What does TikTok's own screen time limit do?

TikTok publishes its own tools under Profile, then the Menu button, then Settings and privacy, then Screen time.3 They are worth turning on, as long as you know what they do.

  • Daily screen time. A daily TikTok screen time limit. TikTok says it will notify you when you reach it, and that you can then close TikTok or enter the on screen passcode to return to it. For people aged 13 to 17 it is on by default at one hour.3
  • Screen time breaks. A reminder after a stretch of uninterrupted use. Tap OK to dismiss it, or Snooze to restart the timer and be reminded again in 10 minutes.3
  • Sleep reminders. TikTok mutes push notifications once your sleep time begins, and works out the end of your sleep time from your age, using at least eight hours for ages 13 to 17 and at least seven hours for 18 and over. When you reach your sleep time you get a notification in the app, which you can dismiss with OK or push back 10 minutes with Delay.3

Notice what none of them do. They notify, mute and remind. They do not stop TikTok opening. If you are already lying in bed, a reminder is one tap away from gone.

Why is it so hard to stop scrolling TikTok in bed?

Part of it is where the app puts you. TikTok says For You is a personalised feed based on your interests and engagement, and that it is the first feed you see when you open TikTok.4 There is no home page to pause on. You land in the feed.

The other part is that the feed does not end. TikTok says its recommender systems select from a large collection of eligible content and rank it by how likely you are to be interested, and that it typically does not recommend content you have already seen.5 So there is no last video, and no natural stopping point to notice.

That is a design problem, not a character problem. More on that in why apps are addictive.

Why a night block beats a daily limit

Night is when the cost lands. In a study of 45,202 Norwegian university students aged 18 to 28, each extra hour of screen use after going to bed was linked to 59 percent higher odds of reporting insomnia symptoms and to 24 minutes less sleep. The link was not significantly different for social media than for other screen activities, so it is the time in bed that matters, not only the app.6

Phones are also right there. In an online survey of 2,005 US adults for the American Academy of Sleep Medicine in March 2023, 87 percent said they regularly sleep with their phone in their bedroom.7 The NHS suggests trying not to use phones, tablets or computers for an hour before bed.8

And limits you cannot wave away do more. In a randomised experiment behind the paper Digital Addiction, the authors report that allowing people to set limits on their future screen time substantially reduces use, and estimate that self control problems account for 31 percent of social media use.9

Block TikTok at night: FAQs

How do I block TikTok at night on my iPhone?

On iOS 27, use Settings, Screen Time, Screen Time Schedule and add a night window. On iOS 26, use Settings, Screen Time, Downtime, set it to Scheduled, and turn on Block at Downtime.

Can I set a TikTok screen time limit inside the app?

Yes. In TikTok, go to Profile, the Menu button, Settings and privacy, then Screen time, then Daily screen time. TikTok says you can enter the on screen passcode to carry on once you hit it.

Do TikTok sleep reminders block the app?

No. TikTok says sleep reminders mute push notifications once your sleep time starts and show a reminder in the app. You can dismiss it with OK or push it back 10 minutes with Delay.

Why can I not stop scrolling TikTok in bed?

The app opens straight into For You, a feed personalised to you, and TikTok says it typically does not recommend content you have already seen. There is no last video to reach.

Is blocking TikTok at night better than a daily limit?

For most people, yes. A daily limit can be used up early and then ignored. A night block covers the hours when scrolling costs you sleep, and it repeats without any decision from you.

Will blocking TikTok at night help me sleep?

It removes one common reason to stay awake. In 45,202 Norwegian students, each extra hour of screen use after getting into bed was linked to 24 minutes less sleep, though that is an association, not proof.
