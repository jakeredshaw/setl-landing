Time Allowances on iPhone, and how to set one that holds

Time Allowances is the setting that used to be App Limits. The feature is better. The number most people choose is what ruins it.

By Jacob Redshaw · 14 September 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

Time Allowances on iPhone is a single daily screen time budget that spans all app categories, which you can then fine tune per category. It replaced App Limits in iOS 27. Set it in Settings, then Screen Time, then Time Allowances, and pick a realistic number.

Time Allowances is what Apple now calls the setting that used to be App Limits. The rename happened in iOS 27, and it came with a real change in how the feature works rather than just new wording.

The short version: instead of setting a separate limit on each app, you set one daily budget that covers everything, then adjust individual categories underneath it.1

Why one budget beats nine limits

Old App Limits had a hole in it that almost nobody noticed. You could be inside every single limit you had set and still have spent five hours on your phone, because the limits did not talk to each other.

Thirty minutes of social. An hour of entertainment. Forty minutes of games. Half an hour of browsing. None of it breached, all of it adding up.

A single daily budget closes that. It measures the thing you actually care about, which is the total.

How to set Time Allowances on iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Screen Time. If you have never used it, turn it on first and let it collect a few days of data before you set anything.
  3. Tap Time Allowances.
  4. Set your overall daily budget.
  5. If one category is doing most of the damage, open it and give it a smaller slice.
  6. Go back to the main Screen Time page and set a passcode. Use one you cannot recall under pressure.

What number to actually pick

This is where it goes wrong, and it goes wrong in the same direction every time. Someone on five hours a day sets a one hour allowance, blows through it before lunch on day one, taps past it, and has stopped looking at the feature by Thursday.

A budget you breach every day is not a budget. It is a notification you learn to dismiss, and learning to dismiss it is the actual harm, because after that you dismiss the real ones too.

Do it this way instead.

  1. Look at your current weekly average first. It is on the main Screen Time page. Do not guess, and do not use the number you would tell a friend. Use the number on the screen.
  2. Take 20 percent off it. On five hours, that is four hours. It will feel pathetically unambitious. That is the point.
  3. Hold it for two weeks. If you are inside it most days, take another 20 percent off.
  4. Repeat until it starts to pinch, then stop there.

Five hours to four is a cut of 365 hours a year. That is nine working weeks. Nobody ever feels impressed by it, and it is worth more than the one hour target you will abandon.

The research supports going for a number you can hold rather than a heroic one. A 2025 randomised controlled trial in BMC Medicine asked 58 students with a baseline of 276 minutes a day to stay under two hours a day for three weeks, and found real improvements in stress, wellbeing, depressive symptoms and sleep quality against the control group.2 But the same trial found screen time climbing back towards baseline once the three weeks ended. A limit only pays while it is in force.

Three things that quietly cancel your allowance

Always Allowed

Anything in the Always Allowed list ignores your settings. Most people put more in there than they remember, usually in the first five minutes of setting it up, and then wonder why the allowance does nothing. Check it.

The passcode you know by heart

If you can type the passcode without thinking, the allowance is advisory. Generate a random one, write it on paper, and put the paper somewhere that takes effort to reach. Or have someone else set it.

The web

Blocking an app does nothing about the same service in Safari. If you allowance Instagram and then scroll it in a browser, you have changed nothing except which icon you tap. Category allowances help here, but check it rather than assuming.

Neither is cheating. Allowances are genuinely useful if the honest problem is that you did not know where the time went. They are weaker if you already know exactly where it went and keep going anyway.

The distinction worth holding onto is between information and enforcement. An allowance is information, delivered at the moment you are least interested in receiving it. That is enough for a surprising number of people, because a lot of phone use really is unexamined rather than compulsive, and simply seeing the number changes it. If you have had the information for months and nothing has changed, more information is not the missing piece.

Try this first: set an allowance for two weeks before paying for anything. If you hold it, you have solved the problem for free. If you tap past it most days, you now know something useful about yourself.

Time allowances: FAQs

What are Time Allowances on iPhone?

Time Allowances is the iOS 27 replacement for App Limits. You set one daily screen time budget that spans all categories, then fine tune individual categories. Find it in Settings, then Screen Time, then Time Allowances.

What is a good daily screen time allowance?

Start by taking 20 percent off your current weekly average rather than picking a round number. A limit you keep is worth far more than an ambitious one you breach and start ignoring within a week.

Why is my Time Allowance not working?

Usually one of three things. Apps in your Always Allowed list ignore it, you know the Screen Time passcode by heart, or you are using the website version of a blocked app in Safari.

Do Time Allowances block apps completely?

No. When the allowance runs out you are told, and you can choose to carry on. That is by design. If you need the app actually gone, you need a blocker rather than a limit.

Where did App Limits go on my iPhone?

It was renamed to Time Allowances in iOS 27 and moved to its own section inside Screen Time. The feature was not removed.

Can I set different allowances for different days?

Allowances are a daily budget. For different behaviour on different days, use Screen Time Schedule, which supports separate weekday and weekend routines.
