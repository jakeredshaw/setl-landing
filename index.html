The SETL app blocker, explained.

What is SETL?

SETL (say it settle) is an app blocker for iPhone that gives you your hours back. SETL Sessions blocks distracting apps on demand, SETL Blocks schedules them across your week, and SETL Sleep locks them at bedtime. How it works.

How does SETL block apps?

SETL is built on Apple's Screen Time framework. You pick apps from Apple's own picker, choose when they lock, and iOS blocks them for you. Calls, alarms and every app you did not pick keep working.

How is SETL different from Apple Screen Time?

Apple Screen Time is free and great for reports, but limits you set for yourself are easy to dismiss. SETL is built to hold the line, in focus hours and at bedtime. Compare them.

Is SETL a screen time restrictor?

Yes. SETL restricts screen time by blocking the apps you choose, during focus sessions, on a schedule and at night. SETL Sessions works as a screen timer blocker. How it works.

How much screen time does SETL save?

In SETL's 2026 beta, the median tester saved 4 hours 3 minutes of screen time a day. Individual results vary.

What is SETL Sleep?

SETL Sleep, often searched as settle sleep, is the bedtime part of SETL. Set your bedtime once and your chosen apps lock automatically every night. See SETL Sleep.

How much does SETL cost?

In the UK, £49.99 a year, which is about £4.17 a month, or £4.99 monthly and £2.49 weekly. Lifetime is £199, paid once. Yearly and monthly start with a 7 day free trial, weekly with 3 days. See pricing.

Can SETL see which apps I block?

No. SETL is built on Apple's Screen Time framework, which gives it private tokens, so we never learn which apps you choose. Privacy policy.
